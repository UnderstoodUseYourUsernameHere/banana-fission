Made in Godot 4

## ChernoBanana

What it is: 
* ChernoBanana, or Banana fission, i dont care which, is a game i figured would fit the idea of "splitting", the circles are meant to represent bananas, and the play area is meant to represent a nuclear reactor. You "split" the bananas, and also split atoms to create energy and heat! Pretty neat, i hope.

## How to use

Itch demo link: https://mustardmustard.itch.io/chernobanana

Or, you can do this:

    Open Godot 4
    Open this project folder
    Press Play (▶) at the top
	
## How to Play

Its very simple, really, you just open up either the web demo or the project itself in Godot, then get to playing! You open up the start screen and find yourself in the game ready to play. Controls are simple:
LMB for igniting, G for a new layout in that level, R to restart that specific level without changing the layout, and T to toggle AUTO-SCRAM in later levels! Y is also there, auto-ignite is not that useful currently, though.

## Gameplay loop

The gameplay loop is rather simple, you click on the bananas, and make sure the chain of reactions isnt *too* hot or else you risk overblowing the Heat limit! 

## Features

ChernoBanana, or however you want to call this game, has the following features:

* Title screen with selection of four levels.
* Sequential progression between levels.
* One initial ignition pulse per attempt.
* Expanding pulses that trigger banana chain reactions.
* Mouse-controlled aiming reticle.
* Highlights showing bananas currently within ignition range.
* Direction indicators for moving bananas.
* Banana movement (they bounce off the chamber walls too!)
* Normal bananas that generate power and heat.
* Overripe bananas that generate more power, more heat, and larger pulses.
* Power targets and maximum heat limits.
* Success, insufficient-power, and overheating outcomes.
* Manual SCRAM to stop an active reaction.
* Retry using the same layout and starting conditions.
* Three-star ratings based on heat efficiency and automation use.
* Energy currency earned from successful reactions.
* Reduced energy rewards for repeated clears.
